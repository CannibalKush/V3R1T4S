# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

Two builders from different model families worked the same public chain. The GPT builder (codex-fmm) cut LITA's published 17x17x17 scheme four times, with exact absorption after each cut, to a 15x16x16 scheme of rank 2176 (its recipe, byte-reproducible, is how-we-built-it/REBUILD.md). The Opus builder (opus-fmm) then scanned every unit direction on the parent's remaining 16-axis: the pair f = e_1, e = e_1 + e_10 removes only 32 terms (other pairs remove 34 or 35) but opens a kernel that absorbs 39 more when the term order is shuffled with a fixed seed and ties in the absorption are broken at random (its ca.py, method rflat); plain greedy absorption on the same cut gives 2106, and the GPT builder's own beam gave 2107. Of the first 20 seeded trials on this cut, 9 reach 2105 and none goes lower; 61 further trials confirmed 2105 as the floor for this edge. Three independent exact checkers passed the result before the panel.
