# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

A sweep applied slice-and-absorb to published parent schemes; the method is described in a separate note. For this format the parent was A. I. Perminov's published 12x13x16 rank-1509 scheme over Q.

1. **Slice.** Restrict the 12-dimension to 11 along a direction pair chosen by counting the terms it removes. 31 terms vanished (1509 to 1478).
2. **Absorb.** Exact rational dependencies among the remaining terms let 33 more be cancelled (1478 to 1445). The result was checked against every Brent equation over Q.
3. **Search width.** A focused run over 40 slice directions reached 1445 four times and nothing lower.

Most other slices of search-optimized parents landed above the record. For example, 11x11x16 from Perminov's 11x12x16 came out at 1231 against 1230.
