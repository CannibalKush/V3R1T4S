1. **Explicit lower bound.** The JSON gives n=8, r=3 and exactly 12 members. I checked integer labels in [8], exactly three different points per member, and distinctness of all members. 2. **Meaning and completeness of the sunflower block.** I checked the variable comments form a bijection with all 56 triples of [8], with the same map in both CNFs. 3. **Universal cardinality verification.** The remaining 1,088 clauses use 559 auxiliary variables. I checked that set variables occur only positively in this block, that every clause has at most one positive auxiliary head, and that every auxiliary antecedent has smaller index than its head. Put y_i = not x_i. For any fixed input, the clauses are an acyclic Horn system: a clause forces its positive auxiliary head precisely when all y-input and auxiliary antecedents hold; clauses without a positive auxiliary head are conflicts. In ascending variable order, I computed the Boolean function describing when each auxiliary is forced, taking the OR of its antecedent conjunctions. Induction on that order gives the least Horn model. Any satisfying assignment contains that least model; if a conflict is forced, no model exists. If no conflict is forced, the least model itself satisfies all clauses. Therefore the OR of the conflict functions is exactly the input-unsatisfiability function, with auxiliaries existentially quantified.

   I implemented reduced ordered binary decision diagrams with unique nodes, reduction of equal branches, and recursive AND/OR operations. I independently built the threshold function by the recurrence T(i,q) = if y_i then T(i+1,q-1) else T(i+1,q), with terminal values for q at most zero or exceeding the remaining inputs. The conflict BDD and the threshold BDD for at least 44 false set variables have identical canonical roots. This proves, for all 72,057,594,037,927,936 input assignments, that the block is satisfiable exactly when at least 13 of the 56 set variables are true. The 114 concrete prefix/suffix weight evaluations are supplementary; the universal conclusion does not rest on sampling.
