For the cardinality block, fix an arbitrary assignment of the 35 inputs. A clause with a true input disappears; otherwise it is a Horn implication between auxiliaries or a negative constraint. In topological order, each auxiliary is forced exactly when at least one of its rule bodies is true. Every satisfying assignment contains all forced auxiliaries, by induction. Setting just those forced auxiliaries true satisfies every positive-head clause. Therefore an extension exists exactly when none of the negative constraints has all its antecedents forced. skeptic/check.py computes that violation function as a ROBDD over the 35 inputs. ROBDD nodes use increasing variable order, hash-cons identical triples, and eliminate identical children; Boolean AND and OR use Shannon expansion. The separately constructed threshold uses a recurrence on position and the number of false inputs still needed. Their canonical root IDs agree. Thus the block is unsatisfiable exactly when at least 23 inputs are false, equivalently when at most 12 are true. This verifies the complete relation under existential auxiliary quantification, rather than just tests of selected assignments.
